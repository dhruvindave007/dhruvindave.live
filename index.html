Office Vision Twin

At Intuitive.ai, 2026

This runs the Office Vision Twin rules in your browser on a simulated office. Badge in, walk people through a door or pick a scenario, and watch a rule raise an incident.

A security digital twin of an office. Six CCTV cameras and badge readers feed a live floor plan, and rules flag tailgating, unauthorised entry and after-hours access. Detection runs locally with YOLO on OpenVINO, with no facial recognition. Amazon Bedrock drafts each incident write-up and a second pass checks it.

The best model passed every check on 22 of 27 eval cases, and the system is covered by 313 backend and 33 dashboard tests. Built with FastAPI and React.

Read the case study →

  • AirfieldShield → Thunderstorm and strong-wind nowcasts for six Indian airfields, trained on 470,057 station-hours of METAR data. Next-hour thunderstorm PR-AUC is 0.576 on held-out 2023–24, against 0.409 for persistence.
  • MusicAI → A music recommender over 43,998 tracks. Top-10 artist hit-rate is 0.565, against 0.165 for random picks. You can try it in your browser on the case page.
  • JanMitra → Case management for police reports in Django REST, with 48-hour escalation through Celery beat. I fixed an access hole and a login lockout; 113 tests.
  • groundcheck → An open benchmark of 123 scenarios that tests whether LLM agents point to the right log lines.
