    Office Vision Twin

    Intuitive.ai · 2026

    A pilot-ready security digital twin. Six CCTV cameras and the badge readers feed a live floor plan of who is in which room, and explicit rules flag tailgating, unauthorised entry and after-hours breaches. Video is processed on premises with YOLO on OpenVINO, with no facial recognition. FastAPI backend, React operator dashboard, and Amazon Bedrock write-ups that a second pass checks against the event records.
